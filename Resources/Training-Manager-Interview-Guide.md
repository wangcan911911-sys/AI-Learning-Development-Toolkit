# Training Manager Interview Guide

## Core Competencies

### Learning Strategy

Questions:

* How do you align training with business goals?
* How do you identify capability gaps?

---

### Program Design

Questions:

* Describe a training program you designed.
* How do you evaluate learning effectiveness?

---

### Stakeholder Management

Questions:

* How do you gain leadership support?
* How do you manage conflicting priorities?

---

### Data Analysis

Questions:

* What learning metrics do you track?
* How do you demonstrate ROI?

---

### AI in Learning

Questions:

* How can AI improve corporate learning?
* What risks should organizations consider?
